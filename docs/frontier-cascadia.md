# Frontier Cascadia — event notes

Source: https://frontiercascadia.org/ (fetched 2026-09-12)

Quoted / public facts:

- High school hackathon, grades 9–12, United States.
- Date: September 12, 2026. 12 hours.
- In-person: UW Foster School of Business / Founders Hall, Seattle. Also live online.
- Free to enter. Teams up to 4, or solo.
- Standard: whether it works. Tangible projects, not slideshows without a demo.
- You may plan, sketch, read docs, and set up an empty repo with boilerplate and dependencies. You may not show up with the project already working. Logic is written on the day.
- Remote: same schedule, submit on Devpost, same rubric. Overall 1st/2nd/3rd are in-person only. Eligible remote projects compete for track awards. Prize eligibility requires US-based participants.
- Schedule: 8:30 doors, 9:00 opening, 9:10 hacking, 3:00 late check-in closes, 5:30 submissions close, 5:45 expo judging, 7:00 finalists, 7:25 demos, 7:55 awards, 8:30 end.
- Tracks / awards: AI for Good, Automated Software Engineering, FinTech, IoT Connected Systems, PNW Impact.
- Partners include Browserbase, Daytona, and Mobbin.
